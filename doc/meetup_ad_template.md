AuraRadar Proximity Discovery: App Testing Session

Are you curious about new tech, privacy, or connecting with people nearby? Come join us for a hands-on physical meetup to test AuraRadar, a new local matching app that securely discovers connections based on physical proximity.

AuraRadar is a privacy-first app designed to help you discover people nearby who share similar interests. Using secure internet-based mesh connections, it safely checks compatibility with people in the same room without revealing your actual location or identity. Since the matching is based on physical proximity, we need to gather in the same physical space to see it in action.

Requirements:
- An Android smartphone running Android 8.0 or newer (with internet access).
- Willingness to join our Google Play closed testing group on-site to download the app.

What we will do:
- Welcome and a brief talk about how private proximity matching works.
- On-site app installation helper.
- Proximity test: walking around the space to see the local discovery and private compatibility matching work in real-time.
- Coffee, discussion, and feedback.
