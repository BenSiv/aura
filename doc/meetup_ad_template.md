## 🎯 About AuraRadar
**AuraRadar** is an offline-first, local-range social discovery application. Instead of relying on central tracking servers or continuous internet connection, AuraRadar utilizes local peer-to-peer Bluetooth and zero-knowledge distance calculations to find nearby users who share matching "resonance interests"—all while preserving absolute anonymity and location secrecy.

Because the app is built on physical proximity networking, **we must test it together in the same space!**
