# AuraRadar Child Safety Standards & CSAM Prevention Policy

**Effective Date: May 18, 2026**

AuraRadar ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to ensuring a safe, respectful, and secure environment for all users within our decentralized social discovery network. We maintain an absolute zero-tolerance policy regarding the posting, transmission, distribution, or storage of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). 

Because AuraRadar operates as a decentralized, local-first proximity networking application, we implement safety standards specifically tailored for P2P protocols to protect children and prevent abuse.

---

## 1. Zero-Tolerance Policy (CSAE / CSAM)

AuraRadar strictly prohibits any form of:
* **Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)**: Cryptographic sharing, indexing, or transmission of images or videos depicting the abuse or exploitation of minors.
* **Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)**: Grooming behaviors, cyber-enticement, solicitation of minors, or any other predatory behavior targeted at children.

Any account or node found attempting to generate, propagate, or link to CSAE/CSAM will be immediately restricted, flagged across the local decentralized reputation mesh, and reported to law enforcement.

---

## 2. In-App Safety & Reporting Mechanisms

To ensure compliance with global child safety standards, AuraRadar implements the following active protections:
* **In-App Reporting**: Every user profile card and discovery chat interface in AuraRadar has a prominent, visible **"Report"** button. Users can immediately report profiles violating child safety standards.
* **Reputation Mesh Flagging**: Reports automatically update the local decentralized "Aura Score" (reputation mesh). Nodes that receive reports of child safety violations are cryptographically flagged and automatically blocked from nearby social discovery sweeps.
* **Immediate Local Ban**: Flagged user nodes are permanently blacklisted on neighboring devices, preventing any further P2P communication.

---

## 3. Mandatory Reporting to Legal Authorities

AuraRadar complies fully with all relevant child safety and reporting laws globally. 
* **NCMEC Coordination**: We immediately report all identified incidents or allegations of CSAM or CSAE to the **National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)** in the United States and international equivalents (such as Interpol and Europol).
* **Local Law Enforcement**: We cooperate fully with regional, national, and international police authorities, providing necessary digital forensics and cryptographic logs generated during local discovery cycles to help prosecute perpetrators.
