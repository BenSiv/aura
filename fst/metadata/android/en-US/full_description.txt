AuraRadar is a decentralized, local-first networking app built to fight the loneliness pandemic by empowering people to connect directly, securely, and privately in the physical world.

No servers. No algorithms. No data harvesting.

HOW IT WORKS

AuraRadar uses your device's local radio (Wi-Fi Direct / Bluetooth) to discover other AuraRadar users who are physically nearby. Profiles are exchanged only when both parties consent to a match, using a cryptographic double-blind handshake so neither side learns anything about non-matching users.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

- Zero-Knowledge Proximity: your exact location is never shared. AuraRadar only reveals that two users are "close", proven via a ZK-Paillier proof, without exposing coordinates.
- Local-Only Storage: all profile data lives on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any central server.
- Store-Carry-Forward Mesh: messages travel hop-by-hop through nearby devices, like a physical peer-to-peer network.
- Open Protocol: the full source code is available under the AGPL-3.0 license.

FEATURES

- Private proximity discovery
- Cryptographic double-blind match handshake
- Decentralized mesh messaging (no internet required)
- Light and dark themes
- No account required, no phone number, no email

AuraRadar is a tool to get you off your phone and into a real conversation.
